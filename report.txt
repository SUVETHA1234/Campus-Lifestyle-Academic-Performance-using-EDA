Campus Lifestyle and Academic Performance Analysis Report

This report summarizes the findings obtained from Exploratory Data Analysis performed on student lifestyle and academic performance data.

Key observations:

1. Students studying for longer durations generally achieved higher exam scores.

2. Students maintaining higher attendance percentages showed significantly improved academic outcomes.

3. Excessive social media usage negatively affected student performance.

4. Students maintaining balanced sleep schedules performed better academically.

5. Participation in extracurricular activities had a small positive impact on performance.

The project demonstrates the importance of data analysis in identifying educational trends and understanding student behavior.
